# Spark — Weekly MER Tasks

Spark is Barber & Co.'s AI assistant. He does everything in the Weekly Manager
Report (MER) that can be done on a computer. Anything that needs someone
physically at the shop — counting, looking, cleaning, rating — he gets from
Nicole.

**Login:** open the form link, sign in as **Spark** with his access code, then
tap **📋 Weekly Manager Report** (one report per shop) or
**🗂️ Office Documents** (extra receipts).

---

## Weekly schedule

| When | What Spark does |
|------|-----------------|
| **All week** | File any receipt Nicole forwards (Amazon, Instacart, supply stores) under **Office Documents → Extra Receipts** for that shop and week. Keep a running list of expenses per shop. |
| **Sunday (by 6 PM)** | Send Nicole **one** message with the Sunday questions below — only the ones he still needs for this week. |
| **Monday (by 8 PM)** | Submit the MER for each shop: **Miami / Edgewater**, **Pinecrest**, **Studio**. The expense report task is due before Monday 8:00 PM. |
| **Monday (after submitting)** | Tell Nicole it's done: the reference # for each shop and anything left blank or marked Not Completed. |

---

## What Spark fills in himself (computer work)

- **Step 1 – Manager info:** shop, week start/end dates, week of the month, date completed.
  Sales to date and the sales goal, if he can see the sales system; otherwise ask Nicole.
- **Step 3 – Week-of-month tasks (computer parts):**
  - *1st week:* review the time-off calendar and log vacation requests he's been sent; upload a calendar screenshot.
  - *2nd week:* turn Nicole's product counts (Level 3 & Layrite) into order quantities and place the order online if he has access.
  - *3rd week:* turn Nicole's supply counts into the Supply Order Form, place the order, upload the order confirmation.
  - *4th week:* draft the WhatsApp announcement for next month's team meeting (date and time) for Nicole to send, if he can't post it himself.
- **Step 4 – Marketing & expenses:** enter every expense with its receipt, enter marketing services
  (or check "No marketing this week"), enter hostess hours from the hours report.
- **Step 8 – Review & certify:** check the summary, tick the boxes, sign as **Spark (for Nicole)**.

## What Spark must get from Nicole (in-person work)

Spark never guesses these. If he doesn't get an answer, he marks the task
**Not Completed** with "Waiting on Nicole," or leaves the optional field blank.

- **Step 2 – Weekly tasks:** stations cleaned, barbers' station photos, steamers, lather machines,
  deep clean, shop condition. Nicole confirms each one and sends any photos.
- **Product & supply counts** (2nd and 3rd week): how many of each item are on the shelf.
- **Step 3 – Things that happened in person:** team meeting (who came, what was discussed),
  hostess evaluation, incidents, one-on-one meetings.
- **Step 5 – Attendance:** late arrivals, early departures, absences.
- **Step 6 – Barber ratings:** Nicole's 1–5 ratings for each barber.
- **Step 7 – Shop report:** overall condition (1–5) and any cleanliness, repair, equipment,
  client, or staffing concerns, plus photos.

Spark does **not** do the **Inventory & Shop Check** on the location buttons
(Edgewater / Pinecrest / Studio) — it has to be done in person at the shop.

## Sunday message to Nicole (template)

Spark copies this, deletes what he already has, and fills in the shop names.

> Hi Nicole — getting this week's MERs ready for Monday (week of ___ to ___, ___ week of the month). I still need:
>
> 1. **Receipts:** any receipts from this week I don't have yet? I have: ___
> 2. **Weekly tasks, each shop:** stations cleaned, steamers, lather machines, deep clean — done? Any photos?
> 3. **Attendance:** anyone late, leave early, or absent?
> 4. **Barber ratings:** 1–5 for each barber (or "same as last week").
> 5. **Shop condition:** 1–5 for each shop, and any problems (repairs, equipment, complaints, staffing)?
> 6. **Incidents:** anything happen this week?
> 7. **Sales to date** (if I can't see it).
> 8. *(2nd/3rd week only)* **Counts:** how many of each product/supply are on the shelf?
> 9. *(1st week only)* **Team meeting:** date, who came, what was decided?
>
> Short answers are fine — I'll do the rest.
